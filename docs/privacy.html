Privacy policy

Last updated: 3 November 2026

Questline is a planner app for Windows, Android and the web browser, made and published as an open-source personal project. This page explains what data it uses and where that data goes. In short: Questline has no servers, and nobody but you can see your data.

Data stored on your device

Your tasks, reminders, events, progress and settings are saved in a file on your own device (on Windows: %APPDATA%\com.questline.app; on Android: Questline's private app storage). In the browser version (ishaan415.github.io/questline/app) they are saved in your browser's storage for that site. The website itself only serves the app's files; it has no server-side code, no analytics and no cookies, and the developer never receives a copy of your data.

Sign in with Google (optional)

If you choose to sign in with Google, Questline asks Google for the first two permissions below. It asks for the Calendar permission only if you later turn on Google Calendar in Settings. Each is used only for the features listed:

  • Your name and email address (openid, email, profile): shown in Settings so you can see which account is signed in.
  • Google Drive, only files Questline creates (drive.file): to keep your progress in sync between your devices and to save daily backups. Questline creates a folder called Questline in your Drive and only ever reads or writes files inside it. It cannot see any other file in your Drive.
  • Google Calendar, only if you turn it on (calendar.readonly, calendar.events): to show your calendar events in the app and, if you choose, to add the events and reminders you create in Questline to your calendar.

Your Google data travels only between your device and Google. It is never sent to the developer or to any other server, never sold, never used for advertising, and never used to train AI models. On Windows the sign-in token is stored encrypted on your device. On Android, sign-in goes through Google Play services on your phone, and Questline keeps only short-lived access tokens in its private storage. In the browser version, Google gives the page a short-lived access token (it expires after an hour) that is kept in your browser's storage for the site and sent only to Google.

Questline's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Spotify (optional)

If you connect a Spotify account, Questline uses Spotify's Web API to show what's playing and to control playback. The token is stored encrypted on your device (in the browser version, in your browser's storage for the site) and is only used to talk to Spotify.

Updates

To check for new versions, Questline downloads a small file from GitHub (github.com/Ishaan415/questline). No personal data is sent.

Removing your data

  • Sign out in Settings → Google account removes the Google sign-in from your device.
  • You can revoke Questline's access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
  • Delete the Questline folder in your Google Drive to remove synced data and backups.
  • Uninstalling Questline and ticking "delete app data" removes everything stored on your device. For the browser version, clear the site data for ishaan415.github.io in your browser's settings.
